Recommended order

  1. Mount the C920 rigidly and lock its focus/exposure.
  2. Calibrate lens distortion with at least ten varied checkerboard photos.
  3. Park the bed at the fixed photography pose and map known XY points.
  4. Place an SVG over the rectified photograph and export G-code.
  5. Validate dry motion before enabling any laser power.
